Operating Principles

The way I approach client work follows a consistent set of principles — the same thinking behind the broader research and systems work I'm developing through London House Group.

Build Systems Before Scaling Complexity

New tools and platforms get introduced only after the underlying workflow is understood and documented. Scaling an undefined process just multiplies the confusion.

Treat Information as an Operating Asset

Data, documentation, and decision logic are treated as infrastructure in their own right, not an afterthought to the pipelines that move them.

Standardize the Repeatable Work

Recurring technical tasks get automated and templated so teams spend their attention on the decisions that actually require judgment.

Design for Long-Term Ownership

Architecture choices account for who maintains the system after the engagement ends, not just how quickly it can be stood up.
